We’re thrilled to bring localization support to the Power Pages plugin for GitHub Copilot CLI and Claude Code. The new /add-localization skill makes it easy to create multilingual Power Pages SPA sites in 43+ languages, including experiences in right-to-left languages such as Arabic and Hebrew.
